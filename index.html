Welcome

Hi, I’m Sophia

Firstly, I want to thank you for taking a moment to view my website. I hope it gives you a glimpse of my passion for capturing the stepping-stone moments of someone's life.

I took an interest in photography at a young age. What started as middle school friends planning a full-blown photoshoot in a corn field, God has since turned into something bigger — a way to reach others and tell the stories of those I wouldn't have met otherwise.

No matter where I am, I have a camera strapped to me. I enjoy all kinds of photography, but most of all, portraits. There is no better feeling than getting the perfect shot and seeing a customer bloom with excitement. My goal isn't just to take photos that will be forgotten about — my desire is to uplift the graduate who is leaving what they know to pursue a new adventure, to encourage the newly engaged couple and remind them of their young love, and to pour into the bride and groom as they devote themselves to one another in an everlasting covenant.

I hope that as you scroll through my page, you feel the love and emotion in each photo — every photo, a piece of your story. If I'm lucky, soon it may be your images telling that same story.

Sophia
