About

Hi, I’m Sophia

The story behind the lens.

Hello again,

I am so thrilled to meet you and learn your story! I hope that by reading a bit of mine, you feel more ease and maybe uncover a shared interest!

As I mentioned, I have loved photography since I was young. That is why I decided to study photography as a minor during my time at THE Ohio State University (yes, the “THE” inclusion is quite important).

Although Columbus served me well, it was time to move home to Louisville, KY after graduation to begin a new adventure. This led me down a very different path than most as I chose to enlist in the Air National Guard. It has been a whirlwind, but I can confidently say the experience has shaped me into a more confident and prepared individual.

In my time outside of work I enjoy staying busy. Working out and staying active have strong holds on my life, as I believe in the many benefits they provide. I am also currently studying to earn a Nutrition Certification to partner alongside my love of fitness. I've been blessed with a wonderful family, so any chance I have for quality time, I'm taking it! Not to forget quality time with my four dogs, Mini, Lily, Scout, and Dixie. It's a lot of dogs, I know.

I truly look forward to our time together, and if there is anything I can assist with to make our session easier for you, please feel free to contact me.

With Love,
Sophia
